# Presets review: furniture, finishes, colours and placement

Reviewed 26 October 2026, as an interior designer, after the layout review and its update. The
presets are in [presets.json](../assets/catalog/presets.json) and described in
[catalog.md](catalog.md); every finish named here is one Muuto sells for that product (the
catalog check makes sure), and every preset's placement passes the layout check in the app.

## The room's own palette

Oak stave parquet (mid honey), white walls and ceiling, white voile sheers, an opal-glass pendant,
oak sills and baseboards, two Hodler lake landscapes (blue, pink, grey-green), and green from the
plants and the trees outside. It is a Wood-and-Metal room in the five-element sense: warm wood,
white and glass. Anything put in it reads against white and oak, in south-south-west daylight
that is bright but rarely direct.

## What was there, and what changed

Eight presets: five looks on today's layout and three layouts. The materials were coherent
within each preset, and the placement in each was sound (every piece sits where the review's
tables put it, pictures centred on what they hang over, the lamp beside the sofa). The weaknesses
were in the set as a whole:

| | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Five of eight sofas were white, grey, beige or sand**, with white chairs in seven of eight. In a white room with oak, that is a pale, safe set with no depth, no dark anchor and no leather. | Four presets added, each on the layout that suits it: a moss green, a cognac leather, a charcoal and a rust. See below. |
| 2 | **Plastic glides on every look's chairs**, felt on the layouts'. On a lacquered oak floor, with chairs pulled out every day, felt is the right specification everywhere (EUR 15 a chair). | Felt glides in every preset. |
| 3 | **The floor lamp was named in some presets and not others**, so picking a look after a layout could leave the layout's lamp standing, or take it away. The default look's note promised a lamp it didn't have. | Every preset says whether the lamp is there and in what colour; every look has it, black (Brown Green in the warm look). |
| 4 | **The Outline 3-seater was in the catalog but in no preset.** The size question (170 or 220 cm) is one the planner should answer. | A 3-seater look on today's layout, in the default fabric, so the two sizes compare like with like. |
| 5 | **The In Situ looks** (Clay 12 grey, Ecriture 210 sand) are fine as they are: the big chaise sofa in two calm neutrals, with the warm one carrying ochre chairs, oak frames, a beige green table and the green lamp. | Unchanged, apart from 2 and 3. |

## Placement

Every preset places every piece, so a preset never inherits where another left something. The
looks share today's placement (the review's measurements); the layouts are the review's, as
updated: the recommended one with the sofa 19 cm off the entry wall and the lamp in the corner
behind its door-end arm, the practice zone across the middle of the room, the pendant swagged
over the dining table. Pictures are centred on what they hang over in every preset; the lamp
stands beside the sofa in every preset (beyond the 3-seater's end, in the corner in the
recommended layout, between the wall and the arm in the view layout).

## What is missing, beyond what a preset can choose

These need new products in the catalog (3D models and option files), not new presets. The
furniture research names candidates for each.

- **A rug** under the lounge group. Nothing softens the oak and the white, and feng shui wants
  the lounge grounded. Muuto's Ply (240 × 170 cm) fits every layout but the practice zone.
- **Storage.** Yoga props, books and the folding chairs have nowhere to go in any preset; the
  layouts assume a low sideboard on the entry wall (Enfold 124 × 45 × 63 cm) that doesn't exist here.
- **A lounge chair** or a second seat. Every preset seats two on the sofa and four at the table;
  a Fiber Lounge in the free corner would give the room a reading seat that isn't the sofa.
- **A pendant to replace the existing one** (a PH 5 or a Strand), so a layout isn't bound to the
  swag hook. The planner swags the existing lamp; it can't yet show another.
- **Curtains** in a colour: the sheers are a fixture, and a linen curtain in a sand or sage would
  change the room more than any sofa.

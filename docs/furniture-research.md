# Furniture that clears the floor for yoga

The yoga floor already exists in this room. In the planner's layout the sofa, the dining table and the tucked-in chairs all end about 2.0 m from the left-hand wall, which leaves a 2.0 m strip along the right-hand long wall, the only wall with no door, window or furniture. The shortlist is therefore about keeping that strip clear, not about folding the dining set away. In the sofa area the pick is the **Muuto Outline 2-seater** (170 × 84 cm) on Muuto's castor base, with a light layer of an **IKEA SLÄKT** folding pouf, a **Fiber Lounge** chair and a **HAY Tray Table** in front of it in place of the 111 cm Outline Pouf, the one piece that reaches into the strip. In the dining area the **Workshop 130 × 65** stays where it stands and gets **Fritz Hansen Series 7** stacking chairs (CHF 487, 4 kg, in stock). For light, a **Louis Poulsen PH 5** hung high replaces the current pendant, whose shade hangs at head height (1.68 m) on the strip's edge, and a **Panthella 160 Portable V3** adds light at floor level. For storage, a wall-hung **String System** unit above the sofa (about CHF 1,280–1,510 for 160 cm) is the pick, with the Swiss **USM Haller Sideboard L** (CHF 1,382–2,210) as the freestanding premium choice. Importing hardly matters now: Switzerland abolished customs duty on furniture and lamps on 1 January 2024, so a purchase from abroad costs only 8.1% VAT plus a carrier fee, and Connox.ch and Finnish Design Shop deliver duty-paid at CHF prices. The choice of shop moves prices far more than the border does: the same Outline 2-seater in Fiord costs CHF 2,830 at Connox.ch and CHF 3,366 on sale at smow.ch. Muuto, Louis Poulsen and USM publish official 3D files that fit the planner's OBJ/GLB pipeline, while IKEA, Westwing and HAY pieces have to be modelled from their dimensions, and no maker's terms found so far allow publishing their models in a public web app. All prices were seen on 26 October 2026; smow's 20% Muuto sofa sale ends on 15 November 2026.

## A 2-metre strip along the right-hand wall is already free

The planner's own data shows where the room's spare floor lies. The sofa stands with its back to the left long wall. The Workshop table stands 20 cm off the entry-door wall and 92.5 cm from the left wall, its long side running into the room, and the tucked-in chairs on its right-hand side end about **2.0 m from the left wall** ([presets](../assets/catalog/presets.json)). That leaves a **2.04 m strip along the right-hand long wall** of the 4.04 m wide room ([room measurements](../src/config.js)), wide enough for an 0.8 m mat with 0.6 m of clearance on each side. The doors shorten it at both ends. The 0.87 m balcony door starts 2.35 m from the left corner of the window wall, and the 0.79 m entry door sits at the right-hand end of the opposite wall. Between the two door swings, **about 3.1 m of the wall stays clear**, enough for a 2.0 m mat with half a metre at each end. That figure assumes both doors open into the room, and it is my arithmetic from the planner's measurements. The room's length is its least certain dimension (±0.15 m), so check it with a tape measure.

Two things already reach into the strip. The **Outline Pouf** in the default preset reaches 2.26 m from the left wall, 22 cm into it, at the window end ([guide](guide.md)). The **existing pendant** hangs from the ceiling outlet in the middle of the room, 2.02 m from the left wall and right on the strip's inner edge, and its 44 cm shade bottoms out at **1.68 m** ([room measurements](../src/config.js)). That is head height, which makes the pendant the one fixed object in the yoga zone. At meals the chairs borrow the strip for their 0.9 m of pull-out space, but only while people are eating. Sofa depth decides how much room is left between the sofa and the strip: the Outline's front stands 1.14 m short of it, room for a coffee table and a walkway. The In Situ configuration 7 the planner models (216 × 169 cm) leaves only 0.29 m, and its preset already places the coffee table 18 cm inside the strip.

## Sofa area: an 84 cm Outline on castors beats a 169 cm In Situ

The **Muuto Outline 2-seater** is the sofa to buy. It is 170 × 84 × 71 cm, with a 40 cm seat height and a 62 cm seat depth ([Muuto Outline fact sheet](https://www.smow.de/pdf/muuto-outline-sofa-factsheets-2023-en.pdf)), and the planner already models it in every Muuto finish. Muuto's configurator also sells it on **black castors** (option C3, EUR 200 extra in the EU shop), and the planner already has a model of that version ([Outline options](../assets/furniture/muuto-outline-soft-2-seater/options.json)). Castors matter because none of the sofas here is light. The Outline 3-seater weighs about **69 kg** ([Connox](https://www.connox.com/categories/furniture/couches-sofas/muuto-outline-sofa-3-sitzer-cognac-silk-leather-verkehrsschwarz-ral-9017-eu.html?itm=180599)) and the Westwing Fluente ships in 72 kg of packages, so wheels are the only way one person can move a sofa to make room for a wider practice. The In Situ loses on depth and weight: the 2-seater in configuration 1 is about **107 cm deep and 81 kg** ([DWR](https://www.dwr.com/living-sofas-sectionals/in-situ-sofa-2-seater/100390763.html?lang=en_US)), and it starts at CHF 3,735 at smow, outside the sale ([smow](https://en.smow.ch/muuto/sofas)). Muuto's Coltre is ruled out for the same reason, with modules 108–160 cm deep ([Coltre fact sheet](https://www.smow.de/pdf/muuto-coltre-sofa-factsheet-en.pdf)).

## Dining area: the 130 × 65 table stays, the chairs should stack

The dining area needs no folding table. A table for four should be about 120–130 × 65–80 cm, or Ø 100–110 cm if round, and **the Workshop's 130 × 65 cm fits that envelope**. With 0.9 m of pull-out space on both long sides it takes about 1.30 × 2.45 m at meals, and tucked in it ends at the strip's edge, as shown above. Round tables are the wrong shape for this room: a Ø 105 cm table with four chairs needs a zone of about 2.85 × 2.85 m (my arithmetic, using the same 0.9 m clearance). The Workshop costs EUR 1,749 in Muuto's EU shop ([Workshop options](../assets/furniture/muuto-workshop-table-130x65/options.json)), and no CHF price was found.

## Light: the pendant is the only lamp in the yoga zone

Lying on the mat, you look straight across at the ceiling pendant, and today's shade hangs at 1.68 m on the strip's edge. Replacing it is the cheapest real improvement to the yoga zone. The **Louis Poulsen PH 5** is the reference pendant that can't dazzle you from below: Louis Poulsen describes its shade as anti-glare ("abat-jour anti-éblouissement"), and Connox calls it completely glare-free thanks to its three shades. It measures Ø 50 × 26.7 cm. Louis Poulsen's Swiss shop sells it for **CHF 1,020** in all lacquered finishes, or CHF 1,280 in copper or brass, with free delivery and returns. Most finishes ship in 4–6 working days, while Hues of blue takes 12+ weeks and brass 4 weeks ([LP CH PH 5](https://www.louispoulsen.com/fr-ch/c/eclairage-interieur/suspensions/ph5)). Connox.ch charges **CHF 731.90–804.90**, mostly from stock ([Connox.ch PH 5](https://www.connox.ch/kategorien/leuchten/pendelleuchten/louis-poulsen-ph-5-ph-50-pendelleuchte.html)). It takes an E27 bulb and dims through an external dimmer, so fit a dimmable 2700 K bulb. Hung on a short cord, its lower edge ends near **2.2 m instead of 1.68 m** (my arithmetic: the 2.50 m ceiling minus the 26.7–28.5 cm shade and the canopy). If 50 cm feels too large, the PH 5 Mini (Ø 30 cm) costs CHF 740.

Among mid-range pendants, the **Muuto Strand closed Ø 45** (CHF 377.90 at Connox.ch, in stock) is the closest size match to today's 44 cm shade ([Connox.ch Strand](https://www.connox.ch/kategorien/leuchten/pendelleuchten/muuto-strand-pendelleuchte.html)). It is 36.4 cm tall and open at the top and bottom, though ([muuto.com](https://www.muuto.com/product/Strand-Pendant-Lamp/)), so the bulb may show from below. &Tradition's **Flowerpot VP7** (Ø 37 × 27 cm, CHF 243.90, 15 colours) and **VP2** (Ø 50 cm, CHF 351.90) are the colourful alternatives ([Connox.ch VP7](https://www.connox.ch/kategorien/leuchten/pendelleuchten/tradition-flowerpot-pendelleuchte-vp7.html); [Connox.ch VP2](https://www.connox.ch/kategorien/leuchten/pendelleuchten/tradition-flowerpot-pendelleuchte-vp2.html)), but whether they hide the bulb from directly below is not documented. The budget pendant is also the flattest: **IKEA BLÅSVERK**, Ø 37 × 18 cm, CHF 29.95, with a glare shield in the box ([IKEA CH](https://www.ikea.com/ch/de/p/blasverk-haengeleuchte-beige-60520903/)).

## Storage: the wall above the sofa costs no floor

Storage cannot go on the yoga wall, and the walls that are left offer only short stretches. On the sofa wall there is about **1.2 m** between the sofa's end and the dining chairs, or about 1.5 m if the pouf goes and the sofa moves to 15 cm from the window wall (my arithmetic from the planner's [presets](../assets/catalog/presets.json)). On the window wall there is the space under the 84 cm sill, where the floor-length curtains and the balcony door's swing compete for room. The wall above the sofa is the one place where storage takes no floor at all, which makes a 20–30 cm deep wall-hung system the natural fit.

The top pick is the **String System**, sold in CHF by smow. Its 75 × 30 cm wall ladders cost CHF 80 each ([smow](https://en.smow.ch/string/string-system-wall-panel.html)). ## Buying: zero duty turns import into a choice of shop

Switzerland abolished customs duty on industrial goods on **1 January 2024**. The abolition covers tariff chapters 25–97, and chapter 94 (furniture and lamps) falls inside that range ([BAZG](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/aufhebung-industriezolle-januar-2024)). Two costs remain on a purchase from abroad. Import VAT at **8.1%** is not collected when the tax comes to CHF 5 or less, which is about CHF 62 of goods ([BAZG](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/regelung-mwst-versandhandel-und-plattformbesteuerung)). The other cost is the carrier's clearance fee: Swiss Post charges **CHF 13 for EU parcels plus 3% of the value, capped at CHF 70** ([Swiss Post](https://www.post.ch/de/empfangen/importverzollung)), and DHL Express charges CHF 19 plus the greater of CHF 3 or 2% of the duties it advances ([DHL CH](https://mydhl.express.dhl/ch/de/ship/customs-services.html)).

Both costs disappear when the shop delivers duty-paid. **Connox.ch** includes Swiss VAT, clears customs itself and charges no clearance fees ([Connox.ch shipping](https://www.connox.ch/kundenservice/versand.html)). It ships from Germany and charges CHF 9.90 for parcels (free from CHF 150), CHF 12 for bulky items (free from CHF 300) and CHF 100 for freight (free from CHF 600). Customers pay up to about CHF 200 to return a freight item ([Connox AGB-CH](https://www.connox.ch/download/AGB-CH-2025-01-08.pdf)). **Finnish Design Shop's** Swiss storefront delivers private orders "fully customs-cleared" with VAT included ([FDS CH](https://www.finnishdesignshop.com/en-ch/product/ph-5-pendant-white-classic)). Small items ship free over CHF 178, and large items cost CHF 50–150 to ship, free over CHF 2,400. Returns are accepted for 30 days, minus a fee. **AmbienteDirect** shows Swiss customers CHF prices including VAT, but does not say it clears customs ([AmbienteDirect](https://www.ambientedirect.com/en/customer-service/shipment-delivery)).

### Swiss shops and Zürich showrooms

**smow.ch** carries most of this shortlist, including Muuto, USM, String, Montana, HAY and Fritz Hansen. Its prices include 8.1% VAT, shipping is free from CHF 99, and paying in advance takes 3% off. Among the brand shops, USM's Swiss shop includes VAT, delivery and installation ([USM shop](https://shops.usm.com/de-ch/shop/usm-haller-sideboard-M51/?colorcode=34)). Louis Poulsen delivers and takes back lamps for free ([LP CH delivery](https://www.louispoulsen.com/de-ch/kundendienst/lieferung)). Vitra's Swiss shop charges at most CHF 30 shipping, plus CHF 20 for delivery into the room with packaging removal and assembly ([Vitra CH](https://www.vitra.com/de-ch/shipping-conditions)). IKEA CH charges CHF 109.95–129.95 for delivery into the room ([IKEA CH](https://www.ikea.com/ch/de/customer-service/services/delivery/)). Pfister carries HAY, &Tradition, Normann Copenhagen, Louis Poulsen, Flos, Montana and Ferm Living, with flat rates of CHF 69 to the kerb or CHF 149 into the flat ([Pfister](https://www.pfister.ch/de/services/lieferoptionen-und-services)). Westwing CH is run from Germany but delivers within Switzerland and allows 30 days for returns ([Westwing CH terms](https://www.westwing.ch/i/terms_and_conditions/)).

Two Zürich dealers work as full-service showrooms. **Teo Jakob** stocks more than 200 brands, among them Fritz Hansen, Horgenglarus and used USM; it asks a 50% deposit on orders from CHF 2,500 and takes returns only as a goodwill gesture ([Teo Jakob terms](https://teojakob.ch/de/agb/)). **wohnbedarf** sells Vitra, USM and B&B Italia. It held outlet sales of exhibition pieces in January and July 2026 and runs an online outlet ([wohnbedarf](https://www.wohnbedarf.ch/en/2026/06/25/wohnbedarf-outlet-design-trouvailles-zu-spezialpreisen/)). Three names are not what they seem: Seipp Wohnen is a German shop on the border, nordicnest.ch now redirects to the German site, and interio.ch redirects to Migros.

### What importing really saves

The worked examples below compare Swiss prices with buying at German RRP, adding Swiss VAT at the ECB rate and the Swiss Post or DHL clearance fee, excluding shipping. In every case a Swiss-facing shop's sale price beats doing the import yourself:

The list-price premium is real, but sale prices are where the money is, and they change weekly. Buying in Germany and driving the piece home has its own rules. Goods up to **CHF 150 per person per day** enter VAT-free; above that, VAT is due on the full value, declared at the border or in the QuickZoll app ([BAZG](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/wertfreigrenze-mehrwertsteuer-einreise-150-franken)). The Vitra Campus has shops, but no outlet was found ([Vitra Campus](https://vitra.com/en-fi/campus/visit)), so the trip only pays off against a promotion.

## 3D models: three brands ship files, the licence is the bottleneck

The planner loads OBJ or GLB models scaled to metres, and it already imports Muuto's OBJ downloads through `scripts/compact-obj.mjs` ([guide](guide.md)). Muuto therefore remains the cheapest brand to add. The Outline Studio, Fiber Lounge, Fiber A-Base, Enfold, Stacked, Leaf, Strand and Post Wall all come from the same download site, in DWG, 3DS, OBJ, SketchUp and Revit, with FBX through pCon ([Muuto professionals FAQ](https://professionals.muuto.com/faq)). Louis Poulsen's files are the richest for a lighting planner. The PH 5 alone has 29 FBX files, one per finish and bulb, plus 30 LDT photometric files, DWG drawings and Revit files ([LP CH PH 5](https://www.louispoulsen.com/fr-ch/c/eclairage-interieur/suspensions/ph5)). The AJ Floor and Panthella Floor have FBX and LDT, and the Panthella 160 Portable has LDT only. The LDT files could drive the planner's lamp light with a real light distribution instead of a guess. The boxes are enough for checking whether a piece fits.

## Conclusion

The research changes which purchases matter. Once the planner's layout is measured, the dining set is not what blocks practice. The obstacles are the two pieces that intrude into the free strip: an oversized pouf and a pendant hanging at head height. A PH 5 on a short cord and a CHF 79 folding pouf do more for the yoga room than any table, and the sofa decision comes down to depth and wheels rather than looks. The one open measurement that can still overturn a recommendation is the mat itself. An 80 cm wide mat rules out every 75–78 cm cabinet on the list and points storage toward Vitsœ's 90 cm bays or an upright basket.

Since 2024, "without import charges" no longer means "Swiss shop". Duty is zero, and duty-paid shops like Connox.ch often undercut both Swiss list prices and a do-it-yourself import. The real spread is between shops selling to Switzerland, such as the CHF 536 gap on one Outline cover, and between a sale and the list price. For the planner, the practical limit is legal rather than technical. Muuto, Louis Poulsen and USM supply files that convert cleanly, but publishing them is a permission question to settle with each maker before the shortlist moves into the public catalog.
